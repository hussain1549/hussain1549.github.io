This paper proposes a reinforcement-learning based Intelligent Stretch Optimisation (ISO) strategy for Information-Centric Networking. The stretch reduction problem is modelled as a Markov Decision Process and solved with a Q-learning algorithm, which explores and exploits routing paths to lower end-to-end latency for 5G-enabled Tactile Internet services.
