This survey reviews Li-Fi and visible light communication (VLC) techniques and the different architectures proposed for high-speed communication using visible light. It compares optical wireless communication with conventional Wi-Fi in terms of security, reliability, speed, and range, and discusses the key challenges in achieving optimal VLC.

Published in the proceedings of ICISCT 2020. Available on [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9080031).
