This work proposes a 6G edge networking design that brings together Information-Centric Networking (ICN) and a Near-Zero Handover (NZHO) mechanism to support ultra-reliable low-latency communication. The approach targets the strict delay and reliability budgets of next generation services through in-network caching and seamless mobility handling.

Status: under review at the IEEE Internet of Things Journal.
