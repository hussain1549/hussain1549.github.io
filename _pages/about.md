I am Qazi Hussain Ahmad, an electronics engineer based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I hold an MSc in Electronics Engineering from the University of Southern Denmark, and I am currently applying for PhD positions in intelligent and ultra-reliable low-latency wireless communication.

My work sits at the meeting point of wireless communication systems, AI-enabled networking, and resilient system design. I build link-level and network-level simulations in Python and MATLAB to study low-latency and ultra-reliable communication for 5G and 6G edge networks. A large part of my research applies reinforcement learning to optimise resource allocation, routing, and handover under interference and changing traffic conditions.

Alongside the research side, I have hands-on industry experience in embedded systems and hardware. This includes building sensor validation systems, writing STM32 firmware in C, designing PCBs, and setting up structured test and data collection workflows. That practical background lets me take communication ideas from a simulation all the way to a working testbed, rather than stopping at theory.

## Research interests

- URLLC and 6G edge networks
- Information-Centric Networking (ICN) and in-network caching
- Edge computing and resource orchestration
- Reinforcement learning for communication optimisation
- mmWave and line-of-sight links
- Resilient communication for drone swarms and defence-relevant systems
