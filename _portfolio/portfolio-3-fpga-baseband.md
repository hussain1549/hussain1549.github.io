Designed and implemented a baseband processing chain on an FPGA platform (Xilinx Spartan series), including modulation and demodulation, filtering, and synchronisation blocks in VHDL.

I integrated the FPGA with an embedded processor in C for control, parameter configuration, and data logging. This enabled real-time experiments and validation of algorithmic assumptions against hardware constraints.

**Tools:** VHDL, C, FPGA (Xilinx Spartan)
