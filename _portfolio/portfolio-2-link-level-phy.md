Built a link-level simulator for multi-antenna wireless systems, implementing modulation, channel coding, and basic MIMO configurations in MATLAB. Selected performance-critical kernels were ported to C++ for speed.

I investigated BER and BLER performance under AWGN and fading channels, and tested adaptive modulation and coding based on instantaneous channel estimates.

**Tools:** MATLAB, C++
